Frequently asked questions

Fifteen answers to the questions we hear most.

Is status.watch free?

Yes. Every feature on the site is free and there's no signup. Enterprise API tiers may launch later — the public API stays free.

Do you have an account or login?

No. Reports and comments are anonymous. We hash your IP to prevent abuse, but we never store your identity.

Where does the report data come from?

User reports, our own uptime probes, vendor status feeds, and public X search — see How it works.

How accurate is it?

We compare live reports against a rolling 30-day baseline. Small services can have false positives; large ones (Instagram, Google) rarely do.

Why is my service marked as down when it works for me?

Outages are often regional. Check the heatmap on the service page — the issue may be affecting other countries or cities.

Can I subscribe to alerts?

Yes — email, RSS, or webhook. Look for the "Subscribe" button on any service page.

Do you have an API?

Yes, public and free — see the API docs. No key required for read endpoints.

How do you moderate comments?

Automated bad-word filter, rate limits, and community flagging. Comments flagged 5+ times auto-hide pending review.

Can I request a new service to monitor?

Yes — contact us. Popular requests get added within a week.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. We don't sell data. Aggregated, anonymized statistics may appear in blog posts.

Do you use cookies?

One functional cookie for your theme preference. No analytics or ad cookies.

Are you affiliated with these services?

No. Every trademark belongs to its owner. We're independent.

Can I embed a status widget on my site?

Coming soon. A small iframe with live status for any service you monitor.

How do I remove a report I posted?

Flag the comment or contact us with the approximate time and location.

Something looks wrong — how do I report it?

Email hello@status.watch or ping us on X.
